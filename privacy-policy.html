Privacy Policy

ERP DECISION — WhatsApp Approval Gateway

Effective Date: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how chrispinkulanga ("we", "us") processes information in connection with ERP DECISION, a WhatsApp approval gateway that connects existing ERP systems.

1. Information We Process

  • Phone numbers
  • ERP user identities
  • Approval request details
  • WhatsApp message IDs
  • Decisions and timestamps

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to deliver requests, identify authorized approvers, update ERPs, and maintain audit records.

3. Sharing

We share information with Meta/WhatsApp for messaging and with the originating ERP for processing.

4. Access

Access is restricted to authorized personnel.

5. Retention

We retain information according to the responsible organization's recordkeeping requirements.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing ruhumbikamtumbajohn@gmail.com.

Some audit records may need to be retained. If so, we will explain the reason to you.
