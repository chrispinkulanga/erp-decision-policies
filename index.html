ERP DECISION

ERP DECISION is a WhatsApp approval gateway that connects existing ERP systems. Authorized managers receive request details and approve or reject them through WhatsApp. The originating ERP owns business permissions and approval workflows. The gateway records decisions and sends callbacks to that ERP.
